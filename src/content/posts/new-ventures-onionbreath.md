The newest venture is not software.

**Onionbreath** — resell onion-themed food under the Onionbreath brand, and migrate the winners to private label. That's the whole model. It exists because the same discipline that runs the fleet and the trim company finally got pointed at a grocery aisle: pick a niche with an identity, keep the entity cheap, keep the stack boring, and let the products prove themselves before anything gets manufactured.

## The shape of it

- **Entity:** Florida LLC — ~$125 to file, $138.75/yr, no state income tax on pass-through. Jordan as own registered agent. Done in fifteen minutes on Sunbiz, or will be the moment the paperwork gets filed.
- **Stack:** Shopify. No custom anything. The site is the least interesting part of this business, which is exactly how a retail site should be.
- **Sourcing:** wholesale and retailer programs first — Faire's 35k+ brands on net-60 terms, concession-supply dealer accounts, a wholesale flavoring house. Net-60 on an opening order means you can sell product before you've paid for it.
- **Hero SKU:** onion cotton candy. Yes, really. It's a real wholesale product, it photographs like a fever dream, and nobody needs it, which is what "viral" is made of.

Year-one cash-out: roughly $2–4K, and the largest line is recoverable float. The biggest fixed cost that isn't Shopify is product liability insurance — which most food suppliers require before they'll ship to you anyway, so it's not optional.

## Why a food reseller

Because the playbook is the same one everywhere else. The trim company's anchor-job lesson — one well-run job pays out six times — is a private-label lesson: resell first, watch what moves, brand the movers. The fleet's lesson — evidence by structure — is an ops lesson: one mailbox on real mail infrastructure, one ledger, receipts for everything. Software ventures, a construction company, now a snack brand. The assets that carry over aren't code. They're the operating habits.

## What's live and what's next

Mail for `info@onionbreath.com` is live end-to-end on real mail infrastructure — SPF, DKIM signed on the wire, verified with an actual send. DNS is parked on Cloudflare until the Shopify store is ready to take the apex. Next gates, in order: file the LLC, get the EIN, file the DR-1 for the reseller certificate, quote the insurance, open the Faire account.

No launch date. The store opens when the entity and the supplier accounts exist, and not before. Announcing a date is how a side venture becomes a deadline with no product.

Onionbreath joins the [ventures](/ventures/) row. DW Trim runs jobs, Curtis ships tools, and now something on the shelf side. Different industries, same ledger.
