The line has been scattered across a plugin directory, two repos, and an npm package. That ends. Everything Curtis converges at **[curtisai.tech](https://curtisai.tech)** — live now. The ledger's own words: "AI chats, kept like records."

[![Curtis — AI chats, kept like records.](/posts/curtis-suite-coming-soon/hero.png)](https://curtisai.tech)

Four pieces, one line. A small amount about each, from the site's own copy:

**Chat.** [Curtis AI Chat](https://curtisai.tech/chat/) — "on your own keys, in your own vault, kept as files." v2.0.1, cut 2026-10-11. 53 built-in carriers, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint welcome. Named agents read, search, and edit your notes when you ask, run on a schedule, spawn swarm specialists, connect to your own MCP tools. "No account with us. No history held hostage." MIT, free core. Install from the Obsidian directory.

**Port.** [Curtis AI Porter](https://curtisai.tech/porter/) — "port your AI chat history anywhere." ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini Takeout, DeepSeek (paste), and `.curt` come in; Curtis records, Obsidian-ready markdown, continuation packs, ChatGPT-shape JSON, and API JSONL go back out. v0.2.0 converts entirely in your browser. Zero network calls, per the page: verify in your browser's devtools. Round-trip verified — files must re-serialize byte-for-byte.

**Audit.** [Curtis AI Audit](https://curtisai.tech/audit/) — receipts for agent activity. "Every AI inference that touches your vault gets an append-only record," sequenced per agent, kept as markdown files you own. Delete a receipt and the gap shows. "It is evidence by structure, not by promise." A hash-chained tier is planned, and not promised before it ships. And to keep two realms apart: curtis-compliance is a separate project. Audit here is receipts, not regulations.

**Data.** [dinfo](https://curtisai.tech/dinfo/) — donated info. "Instead of companies quietly taking your data, you give it — on the record." Every donation numbered, receipted, and voidable. Metadata only, extracted on your device; your words never leave it. There is no code path that stores text. Portal and Obsidian plugin are live as a local-first beta. The shared pool opens with v2 once the legal layer is signed.

The line doesn't stop at four: curtis ai crm is built — contacts, deals, interactions as markdown, not yet in the directory — and curt files are live, one-file pages readable anywhere.

One mark, four verbs. [curtisai.tech](https://curtisai.tech).
